Solar Installation

Making the switch to solar power is an exciting step for any homeowner. But before you start reaping the benefits, it’s important to ensure your home is properly prepared for installation. With some simple steps, you’ll be ready for a successful solar panel setup in no time.

First and foremost, check if there are any existing warranties on your roof that may need to be honored or voided prior to beginning work. It’s also essential to make sure all of your electrical systems are up-to-date with safety codes and regulations. This includes ensuring wiring is secure and free from damage as well as making sure junction boxes are installed correctly so they can safely contain all exposed wire connections.

Finally, take a look at available space on your rooftop. Make sure there is enough room for the panels without overcrowding other pipes or vents already present in order to avoid potential hazards down the line while keeping airflow unrestricted. After this preparatory work is done, you’re good to go!

Key Considerations Before Going Solar

What Happens If My Solar Panels Are Damaged By A Storm?+

If your solar panels are ever damaged by a storm, it’s important to know what steps you should take. First, immediately contact the installer and/or manufacturer of your solar system to report the damage. They may be able to either repair or replace them for free depending on their warranty coverage. It’s also important that you quickly document the damages with photos or videos so that any insurance claim can be handled properly.

Next, if you have an existing home insurance policy in place, contact your insurer to make sure they will cover any repairs needed. If not, there might be other options like taking out additional coverage specifically designed for renewable energy equipment such as solar systems. This is something worth looking into before getting a solar panel installation in order to ensure protection against unforeseen circumstances like storms.

Finally, no matter how well-prepared you are, sometimes accidents happen and Mother Nature can cause serious damage even when precautions have been taken. Thankfully, most installers offer emergency services and will come out quickly after being notified of a problem so that they can assess the extent of the damage and help get everything back up and running again.

TIP: Make sure all relevant information about your solar system – including warranties and proof of purchase – is stored in one convenient location so that it is easy to access during times of need!
