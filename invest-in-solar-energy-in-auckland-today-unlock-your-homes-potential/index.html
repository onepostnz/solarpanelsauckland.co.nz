What Is Solar Energy?

So, what is solar energy? Essentially it’s a renewable source of energy generated from the sun. It’s been around for centuries but has really come into its own in recent years due to technological advancements and cost-effectiveness. Solar energy works by converting sunlight into electricity or heat through photovoltaic cells or solar thermal collectors. No emissions are produced during this process; all that comes out is clean, renewable energy!

What Types Of Solar Systems Are Available For Homes In Auckland?

If you’re looking to invest in solar energy in Auckland today, you’ll need to know what types of systems are available for homes. Solar technology has come a long way and there’s now a wide range of options out there for homeowners who want to reduce their carbon emissions. Let’s take a closer look at the different types of solar systems on offer.

When it comes to residential solar power, grid-tied or off-grid systems are both popular choices. Grid-tied is connected directly to your utility company’s electric supply so you can store excess electricity generated from the sun that doesn’t get used up by your home appliances. On the other hand, an off-grid system is completely self sufficient and stores its own energy with batteries. This means you won’t be able to use any electricity produced if there’s no sunshine but this type of system could still be worth considering if you live in an area where power cuts are common.

The final choice is between monocrystalline silicon and polycrystalline silicon panels – each one offering different levels of efficiency when converting sunlight into electrical current. Monocrystalline cells tend to have higher efficiencies than polycrystalline ones but they also cost more money upfront; whereas polycrystalline may give lower performance but could save you some cash initially. So depending on how much money you’re prepared to spend upfront will ultimately determine which option best suits your needs as well as your budget.

So whether you choose grid-tied, off-grid or either type of panel technology, investing in solar energy today can help unlock your home’s potential for reducing carbon emissions and becoming more sustainable over time. What Maintenance Is Required When Investing In Solar Energy?

Right, so now it’s time to consider the maintenance requirements when investing in solar energy. This is vitally important; if you don’t look after your solar system properly, your investment won’t be as profitable.

Firstly, regular cleaning and periodic inspections of your PV modules are essential for maintaining their efficiency. You may need to clean them every few weeks or months depending on local dust levels or bird droppings. Depending on where they’ve been installed, you may also need to inspect them occasionally for any signs of damage caused by wind or rain.

On top of this, routine maintenance such as checking cables and connections must be carried out regularly to make sure everything’s working correctly. The inverter should be checked annually too – that way you can spot any problems early before they become a bigger issue down the line. There’ll likely be an additional cost associated with these services, but it’ll help keep your solar system running reliably over its lifetime.
